Suyo App — Geometry & Performance Update v12
============================================

Scope
-----
The original outer squircle renderer is preserved. N=3, 14 segments per corner,
the original 0.01 CSS-pixel path serialization, and clip-path:path() remain unchanged.
This update improves the mathematical validation of the inward offset and reduces
repeated geometry work through bounded caches and a nested-shape index.

Corner geometry: why the miter is retained
------------------------------------------
For a convex polygon P, the exact inward Euclidean offset at distance d is the erosion
P ⊖ B(d): the intersection of all inward half-planes shifted by d. Its boundary is
formed by parallel edge lines and their intersections (miter vertices).

A circular/round join is appropriate for an outward polygon offset. Substituting a
round join for this inward offset would not keep the child boundary at distance d from
the parent boundary and would create a different, locally shallower inset.

Therefore v12 does not replace the miter with a cosmetic round join. Instead, it adds
an explicit Euclidean distance validation at every inset vertex and every inset-edge
midpoint, in addition to convexity, half-plane, collapse, and containment checks.
This checks the actual corner geometry rather than judging the join by appearance.

Important distance distinction
------------------------------
The child-to-parent boundary distance is the defining inward-offset distance d.
The reverse parent-to-child distance is not generally d at a polygon corner. For a
corner with a nonzero turning angle, the original corner can be farther from the
inset boundary than d. This is an inherent directional-distance property of inward
offsets, not an error that a round join can remove while preserving the same edge gap.
The browser test reports both directions and the vertex-only measurements separately.

ui.js in Node.js. Parent vertices are rounded to the same 0.01px precision used by the
original clip-path; child path coordinates are serialized to 0.0001px.

Performance optimization
------------------------
- Polygon sample points are cached by width, height, and corner radii.
- Inward-offset vertices are cached by parent polygon identity and offset distance.
- Final nested child paths are cached by parent geometry, child dimensions, and insets.
- Each cache is an LRU Map capped at 96 entries to prevent unbounded growth.
- A WeakMap-based nested-shape index replaces scanning every tracked shape for each
  ResizeObserver entry. Only the changed shape's nested descendants are repainted.
- ResizeObserver batches use a Set to avoid repainting the same descendant more than once.

Isolated Node.js benchmark
--------------------------
10,000 repeated calculations of the same 320 x 32 / radius 9 / inset 2 geometry,
60 vertices, three runs:
- v11 uncached: 4,969.3 / 5,035.3 / 4,658.1 ms.
- v12 warm cache: 24.3 / 22.3 / 26.3 ms.
- Median comparison: approximately 204x faster for this repeated-identical-geometry
  microbenchmark.
This is not a whole-app or device benchmark. First-time geometry creation still incurs
its full calculation cost; the benefit applies when geometry is reused.
